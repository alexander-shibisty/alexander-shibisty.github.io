Privacy Policy for "Bookmarks 2 - Bookmarks Checker" Effective Date: August 26, 2026 1. Introduction "Bookmarks 2 - Bookmarks Checker" is a browser extension designed to check the HTTP status codes of your bookmarks and help you manage them efficiently. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our extension handles data. 2. Data Collection and Usage We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or bookmark data to any external servers. All operations, including bookmark checking and status reporting, are performed entirely locally within your browser. 3. Use of Permissions Our extension requests the following permissions from the Chrome browser, strictly for local functionality: - bookmarks: To read your bookmark tree and check the HTTP status of the saved URLs. - storage: To save your local preferences (e.g., theme, language, check intervals) and the UI state of the extension. - tabs: To detect if the currently active tab's URL exists in your bookmarks and highlight it accordingly. - sidePanel: To provide an optional, larger interface for easier bookmark management and searching. - alarms: To schedule and perform periodic background checks of your bookmarks at your chosen interval, even when the extension popup is closed. 4. Third-Party Services This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking services, or external APIs. No data is shared with third parties. 5. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. 6.
